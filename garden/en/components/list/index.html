Icon List (list)

Pair line icons with short observations in a compact card, or add headings and multi-paragraph notes for divided steps.

  • When to use: Listing parallel actions, signals, or practices paired with an icon, or grouping titled steps with one or more paragraphs each.
  • When to skip: Items follow a strict numbered sequence or chronological order; use a standard ordered list or timeline instead.

You can tell the AI:

```garden
List the four things a newcomer usually does before signing up, each paired with a small icon.
```
