Bar Chart (chart)

Render proportional horizontal bars with a built-in expandable data table underneath.

  • When to use: Comparing the relative magnitudes of 2 to 8 values that share the same unit.
  • When to skip: Numbers change with reader input (use interactive), or you already have a Markdown table with the same numbers in that section (the chart includes its own expandable table).

You can tell the AI:

```garden
Compare the max widths of the prose column, wide figures, and wide tables with a horizontal bar chart.
```
